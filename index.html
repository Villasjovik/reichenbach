Four tracks

Three ways to put Reichenbach in front of the home buyer.

Track 01

The Hand-Thrown Pot, at Home

"The quiet handmade piece, made visible"

A campaign built around the Majah pot — the plain white, hand-thrown pitcher that carries the whole story of the manufactory in one calm object. The idea is to let a single made-by-hand piece breathe on a real Nordic table by the water, where its undecorated form reads as craft rather than product. The purpose is desirability; the goal is end-customer reach for a piece whose value is felt, not explained.

Hand-thrown · Reach · Desirability

Track 02

Made in Germany, Felt in the Home

"Heritage that does commercial work"

Reichenbach's whole value rests on provenance — thrown and finished by hand in Thuringia since 1900. The idea is to make that invisible craft visible to the people who actually buy it: the hand-turned pieces shown not as a catalogue but as a calm, lived-in Scandinavian setting where the mark of the hand reads. The purpose is premium positioning; the goal is to justify the price point to end-customers and pull demand through to retail.

Premium · Provenance · Retail pull-through

Track 03

From the Shelf to the Table

"Opening the consumer channel"

Trade and gastronomy buyers already know Reichenbach; the harder customer is the private end buyer who has never seen the porcelain in a real home. A campaign that bridges that gap — the hand-turned plate and footed pedestal bowl set on a calm, laid table in a setting the home audience aspires to. The goal is direct shop traffic that converts, plus a content package you own and reuse across your own site, newsletter and socials long after the campaign runs.

Consumer reach · Owned content · Conversion

Track 04

Your pick

"Built around your priority"

If there is a specific piece you want to put forward — the Majah pot, the hand-turned plate, the footed pedestal bowl, or another form from the hand-thrown line — name it and the campaign is built around it. You know which piece deserves the push better than anyone.

Tailored · Your edition · Your call
